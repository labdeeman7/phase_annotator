P5

Gallbladder packaging

Begins when a retrieval bag is purposefully introduced, opened or positioned to contain the detached gallbladder.

P6

Cleaning and coagulation

Begins when inspection, irrigation, suction, clearance of blood/fluid, or haemostasis becomes the principal activity rather than brief support for dissection.

P4 → P6: V37-B04

Open clip separately
P7

Gallbladder retraction (specimen extraction)

Begins when the contained specimen is purposefully engaged and sustained withdrawal from the operative field begins. Ordinary traction during P1, P2 or P4 is not P7.
