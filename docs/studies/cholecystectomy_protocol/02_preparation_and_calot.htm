P1

Preparation

Includes survey, exposure and retraction, adhesiolysis, and removal or division of tissue whose immediate purpose is to expose the gallbladder or obtain access to the operative target.

Remain in P1 during general exposure, clearing fat or inflammatory tissue, and hook use outside focused hepatocystic-triangle dissection.

Interim gallbladder-drainage rule

An inflamed or tense gallbladder may be purposefully punctured to release pus, bile, or other contents. Label the complete purposeful drainage episode Undefined and add the exact note Gallbladder drainage — candidate phase. Return to the appropriate seven-class phase when drainage ends.

This preserves a candidate real-world phase for later review; it does not mean the clinical action is unknown. Do not apply the rule to incidental puncture or leakage.

P2

Calot triangle dissection

Begins when focused dissection within the hepatocystic triangle, intended to expose or identify the cystic duct and/or artery, becomes the sustained operative objective.

OAUTHC adaptation

Hook appearance alone does not determine the P1-to-P2 boundary.

Inflamed and adhesed cases may require prolonged hook adhesiolysis or exposure before true Calot dissection. Use anatomy, tissue target, direction of dissection and sustained intent together.
