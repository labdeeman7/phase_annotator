P3

Clipping and cutting

Begins when dissection gives way to purposeful occlusion of the cystic duct or artery with a clip applier, ligature or equivalent method. Continue through division and brief confirmation that division is complete.

P2 → P3: V50-B02

P4

Gallbladder dissection

Begins after clipping/cutting has finished and purposeful separation of the gallbladder from the liver bed begins. Brief haemostasis supporting continuing separation remains P4.
