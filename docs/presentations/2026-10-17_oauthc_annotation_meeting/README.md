# OAUTHC Surgical Workflow and Annotation Meeting

Meeting: **17 October 2026, 11:00 am**

Open `OAUTHC_Surgical_Workflow_and_Annotation_Meeting.pptx` in PowerPoint. The presentation is designed for clinicians and student annotators who may be encountering surgical artificial intelligence for the first time.

Supporting files:

- `SPEAKER_NOTES.md`: slide-by-slide delivery notes and discussion prompts.
- `FIGURE_SOURCES.md`: provenance and reuse information for paper figures.

The intended session combines the slides with two live elements:

1. show one Cholec80 and one de-identified OAUTHC P1-to-P2 example on the dedicated comparison slide; and
2. demonstrate Phase Annotator using a safe training video.

Do not place identifiable clinical screenshots or video inside the committed presentation. Keep the clinical examples in the authorised local environment and play them separately.

The presentation introduces Cholec80 before using it, treats the Preparation-to-Calot observation as an early hypothesis to examine across 26 available videos, and explains a staged workflow: five shared videos for inter-rater variability followed by divided batches of 10, 10 and 6. It also validates the concern about prolonged inactive periods while recommending that existing footage be preserved and inactivity handled without silently compressing the canonical timeline.
